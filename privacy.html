Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how VODHunter Uploader ("the app") handles information. The app is an internal tool operated by VODHunter to publish our own video clips to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Information the app accesses

When we connect one of our own accounts, the platform gives the app an access token and basic account details (account ID, username or channel name). The app uses them only to upload videos we have approved and to read back the link of each published video.

How information is stored

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a private folder. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data from other users

The app is not offered to the public. It does not collect, store or process personal data from viewers or from any other user.

Google API Services

VODHunter Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok and Instagram

Uploads to TikTok and Instagram use their official APIs and follow the TikTok Developer Terms and the Meta Platform Terms.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions), TikTok settings, or Instagram settings. To ask for deletion of any stored data, email us; we delete it within 30 days.
